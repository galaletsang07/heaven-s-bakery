Establishment of Heaven's Bakery

Kelebogile Sento, founder of Heaven's Bakery.

Heaven's Bakery was founded in 2016 by Kelebogile Sento, a 17 year old back then who had a dream of baking for the world. Her dream was to bring freshly baked goods to every family in her community and beyond, creating moments of joy and togetherness through food.

What started as a small business in her high schools days, to selling door-to-door, grew into a well-loved local bakery known for delicious cakes, muffins and pastries.

Every recipe is prepared with care, love and high-quality ingredients to ensure customers enjoy the best bakery experience possible.

Missions and Goals

Our mission is to create fresh baked products using wholesome ingredients while creating happiness through food.

We aim to expand our bakery services, support the community and continue delivering unforgettable flavours and memories to customers every day.
