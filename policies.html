Allergy & Dietary Information

Heaven's Bakery takes allergies and dietary restrictions seriously. Please do notify us of any allergies or dietary requirements when placing an order. We handle common allergis including, nuts, eggs, dairy, soy and glutten. We also follow careful preparation practices but we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. Vegan, vegetarian, glutten-free and other specialty options are available upon request, but may require additional lead time for preparation.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Full payment must be completed before custom orders are processed. We accept cash, card payments, and mobile payment methods approved by the bakery. For large celebration cakes and catering orders, a deposit may be required before work begins. Refunds are only issued in cases where the bakery is unable to fulfill the confirmed order. Orders may be cancelled atleast 24-28 hours before the scheduled collection or delivery for a full/partial refund. Cancellations made after this period do not qualify for a refund.

Custom Order Policy

Custom cakes and specialty orders should be placed several days in advance to allow our bakers enough preparation time. Customers are responsible for providing accurate spelling, colors, themes, and design requests before confirmation. Once preparation has started, major changes to the order may not be possible. Heaven's Bakery reserves the right to decline designs that cannot be completed within the requested time.
